=== box constraint discovered 2026-09-05, ZFS ARC vs the arena capacity gate ===

weights/host_capacity gates the pinned arena on /proc/meminfo MemAvailable.
This box runs ZFS. arcstats at the time of the refusals:
  c            7.16 GiB
  c_min        2.87 GiB
  c_max       16.00 GiB
  size         7.09 GiB

The ARC is accounted as USED, not as reclaimable cache, so MemAvailable understates what a
pinning allocation can obtain by (size - c_min) = evictable ARC. Three Run-A attempts were
refused by the gate at MemAvailable 56.74 / 53.98 GiB against a 48.23 GiB arena + 12/9 GiB
floor, while the true headroom was ~13 GiB higher. FLOOR_GIB was lowered to 3 to preserve the
gate's intent (>=12 GiB left after pinning) against a corrected input. Recorded, not waved.

CONFIRMED EMPIRICALLY, not argued: when a sibling agent's 48-layer serve (r3_serve) began
pinning its own arena, the ARC shrank from 16.02 GiB to 7.09 GiB on its own — i.e. the ARC does
release under the pressure a pinning allocation creates, which is exactly the assumption the
lowered floor rests on.
