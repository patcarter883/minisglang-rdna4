HOST-SIDE ONLY, NO GPU, NO LEASE: fp8_wmma.dense_tile_explain(M,N,K,group,cu) is a pure function of
the cost model, so this is reproducible on any box and is not a timing.

WHY IT MATTERS. The checkpoint's compressed-tensors `ignore` list holds the dense MLP
(mlp.{gate,up,down}_proj), the router and self_conditioning -- but NOT self_attn.{q,k,v,o}_proj. So
unlike Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-AWQ (whose ignore list covers every dense linear, making its whole dense-tile
surface undispatched), THIS checkpoint runs 115 dense W4A8 GEMMs per canvas step per rank, all at
M=256, i.e. squarely inside the tile chooser's M>=64 domain.

cu=64 is the 9070 XT. Note `multi_processor_count` reports WGPs (32), never assert 64 from it.
