--- dense_tile_selection.txt -------------------------------------------------------------------
HOST-SIDE ONLY, NO GPU, NO LEASE: fp8_wmma.dense_tile_explain(M,N,K,group,cu) is a pure function of
the cost model, so this is reproducible on any box and is not a timing.

WHY IT MATTERS. The checkpoint's compressed-tensors `ignore` list holds the dense MLP
(mlp.{gate,up,down}_proj), the router and self_conditioning -- but NOT self_attn.{q,k,v,o}_proj. So
unlike Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-AWQ (whose ignore list covers every dense linear, making its whole dense-tile
surface undispatched), THIS checkpoint runs 115 dense W4A8 GEMMs per canvas step per rank, all at
M=256, i.e. squarely inside the tile chooser's M>=64 domain.

cu=64 is the 9070 XT. Note `multi_processor_count` reports WGPs (32), never assert 64 from it.

           --selected-regions --stats, 40 canvas steps, window canvas_step#20..#60)
perf lvl : AUTO on both legs. No counter run was mixed in; `profile_standard` pins clocks non-boost
           and its absolute times belong to a different surface.

TWO LEGS, TWO INSTRUMENTS, ON PURPOSE. MINISGL_CANVAS_TIMING costs two torch.cuda.synchronize() per
step and serialises the overlap the trace measures; rocprofv3 stretches the step (139.15 ms traced
vs 97.70 ms un-profiled = 1.424x). So the ABSOLUTE step comes from the base leg and the SHARES come
from the trace, and the deflated `real` column is stated as derived, not measured.

OCCUPANCY WITHOUT --pmc. rocprofv3 --kernel-trace already emits VGPR_Count, Scratch_Size,
LDS_Block_Size, Workgroup_Size and Grid_Size per dispatch, so waves/SIMD follows from the gfx1201
law -- min(16, 1536 // (ceil(VGPR/24)*24)) -- with no counter run, no ROCm 7.14 image, and no
profile_standard window. That is where every occupancy number in step_breakdown.txt comes from.
What it does NOT give is the VALU/VMEM/LDS issue split; that still needs --pmc.

ONE RANK. A TP=2 serve traces both ranks through the inherited LD_PRELOAD; this trace carries a
single agent, and every per-step figure is therefore one card's work, which is what a per-step cost
should mean.
