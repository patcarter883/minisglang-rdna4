TWO MEASUREMENT CONTROLS THAT WERE NECESSARY, NOT COSMETIC
  1. CACHE-BUSTED PROMPTS. tools/serve_matrix_bench.py reuses one fixed long prompt for every
     prefill cell, so after its own warmup every prefill is a RADIX PREFIX-CACHE HIT and its TTFT
     is a cache lookup, not a prefill. This A/B is entirely about prefill GEMM cost, so
     tools/ab_tile_serve_bench.py prefixes every single request with fresh random hex.
  2. PINNED KV POOL. Left to auto-size, the pool is whatever VRAM survives the model plus the
     recurrent-radix/GDN/graph reservations — on a 16 GB card that is the SCRAPS (0.02 GiB), and
     the two images' kernel packages differ in code-object footprint by enough to swing it 42%
     (BEFORE 2448 vs AFTER 1424 servable tokens; see unpinned_kvpool/). A different pool changes
     which requests are ADMITTED, so that run compared admission policy, not GEMM cost. --num-pages
     makes the pool a constant. (The 42% swing is itself a real cost of the larger tile lattice.)
  Also --no-gdn-radix: the recurrent-radix snapshot store clones per-request GDN state from the
  torch allocator beyond its own accounting reservation, and it killed BOTH Qwen legs at the same
  point in rep 3 with `torch.OutOfMemoryError ... gdn_state.clone_slot` (see
  discarded_run_gdn_radix_oom/). It is a stochastic, history-dependent allocator inside a
  measurement of GEMM cost, and because this bench cache-busts every prompt it can never register a
  hit anyway. Off, it is pure removed variance. Inert for MLA/GLM.

WHICH MODEL EXERCISES THE CHANGED KERNEL — the load-bearing finding
    The engine's own hip-engage ledger, per leg:
      QWEN dispatches  fp8_wmma.dense_bf16_gemv[gdn_proj]  and the MoE gemms — and NO
           fp8_wmma.mmq_fp8_gemm(...) AT ALL. cyankiwi/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-AWQ-4bit's
           modules_to_not_convert covers every linear_attn.*, every self_attn.{q,k,v,o}_proj and
           every mlp.shared_expert.* projection, i.e. the checkpoint leaves ALL dense linears in
           bf16 and quantizes only the routed experts. The W4A8 dense GEMM the tile work optimizes
           is therefore never called on the production model.
      GLM  dispatches  fp8_wmma.mmq_fp8_gemm(wmma_tiled_tuned)  (prefill) and
           fp8_wmma.mmq_fp8_gemm(decode_gemv)  (decode). QuantTrio/GLM-4.7-Flash-AWQ excludes only
           mlp.gate., model.layers.0. and self_attn, so its MoE-shared/dense MLP linears ARE W4A8.
    GLM is thus the only leg that can see the dense tile at all, and it is also the model carrying
    the one known isolated regression (glm.gate_up tp2 M=512, 0.8141x).

RESULT (medians of 3 reps; full numbers in comparison.txt, raw repeats in the JSONs)
    QWEN  every cell within 0.7%, no cell outside run-to-run spread. Flat, as predicted by the
          engage ledger.
    GLM   flat everywhere EXCEPT prefill at ~516 prompt tokens, where AFTER is 2.4% SLOWER on TTFT
          (192.17 -> 196.80 ms) with DISJOINT ranges ([191.87-193.41] vs [196.43-197.53]).
          That is the recorded glm.gate_up tp2 M=512 0.8141x surfacing end-to-end, diluted from
          22.8% on the kernel to 2.4% on TTFT. chooser_tile_vs_M.txt shows why and how narrow it
          is: at M=448..512 on a 64-CU card the chooser takes 256x64 over the old hardwired
          256x128 on a claimed 1.036x — a near-tie in the model, and 1.228x the WRONG way in
          reality. On the 56-CU card it takes 256x128 at those M, so at TP=2 the two ranks pick
          different tiles and the slower rank gates the step.
    Decode is flat on both models at M=1/4/16 (<=0.7%), which is what "serving is overhead-bound"
    and "bs=1 decode is inter-kernel-gap bound" predict.
